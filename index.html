G.U.I.D.E. Player + operator sides shipped React · TypeScript · Supabase · Foundry VTT

New · Foundry VTT module

A character-management web app for my tabletop campaign. Players get their character sheet, I get an operator console. In the story it's a helpful neural implant. It is also, secretly, the villain.

Now talking to Foundry

A Foundry VTT module links the virtual table and the app. It's still a work in progress, but most of it works.

  • Operator consoleplannedshipped
  • HPapp onlysynced both ways with the VTT
  • VTT eventsnonetrigger events in the app

Players spend the whole game inside an interface that is quietly watching them. The player side is cyan and reassuring. Amber is the system-truth colour, and it leaks through when something is wrong. The operator console is amber all the way down, because that's where the truth lives.

How I work

I finish what I start. Leaving something half done breathes down my neck until it's done.

If something needs a deep fix or a deep dive, I'll do it. I like looking at systems from the inside: how they work, and why they don't.

I sweat the details and ask a lot of follow-up questions. If something's missing, I ask what it needs, then build that too.

“I like systems I can take apart, understand, and rebuild better.”
