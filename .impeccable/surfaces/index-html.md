# Surface: index.html (home, single page)

Mode: Experience. The work leads from the first viewport.
Audience: fellow devs and modders, plus curious strangers. This is a personal page, not job hunting; there is no hiring language.
Must keep: the G.U.I.D.E. SVG wordmark, the Skills section (in some form) and the live palette.
Avoid: anything that reads corporate.
Copy may be rewritten in Sam's voice.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the ring binder Sam brings to the table. Each project gets a tabbed section and the newest work is clipped in front. It refuses the category default of a split hero followed by equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: The cover is dark violet vinyl, #14111e. Pages are lilac-white paper (#f5f0ff) with a faint periwinkle grid. The tab dividers are flat Berry blocks: pink #ff5d8f, lilac #b98bff, periwinkle #7c8bff, cyan #46d3ff, orange #ff9f43. They have no tints or gradients, only one hard paper edge. Dymo-tape labels are reversed caps on #14111e tape. Screenshots and the video sit in clear sheet protectors with a punched-hole margin. Margin notes are set in Recursive Casual, body text in Recursive Sans, and stack/status cells in Recursive Mono. The type scale has two sizes plus a display; rank comes from weight, case and reversal. Every project sheet uses the same header strip: tab colour, number, status cell and stack line. Changes are shown as before → after lines. The newest entry is the most saturated, and older sheets are quieter.

STORY: A visitor sees G.U.I.D.E. running before reading anything, learns that it now talks to Foundry VTT, then flips through the other projects, the toolkit and who Sam is. If they want to talk, they find the email at the back.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Desktop: an open binder fills the viewport. The left inside cover holds Sam's avatar clipped on, the name, a one-line intro and a margin note. The right page is the G.U.I.D.E. sheet, with the wordmark and the autoplaying muted demo video in a sheet protector at about 60% of the page width. A Dymo strip reads "NEW · FOUNDRY VTT MODULE" and before → after lines sit beneath.
- Tab rail: on the right edge, coloured tabs (G.U.I.D.E., Projects, Toolkit, About, Say hi) are the nav.
- Phone: the cover stacks above the sheet, and the tab rail becomes a bottom strip.

FORM: The DM's Binder, assigned (grounded candidate #3 on a re-roll). Seed key 54f61ee1, reroll 1. Code-led. Signature interaction: the tab rail. Clicking a tab flips to its section, and the active tab stands proud of the page edge. Motion is a short page-flip/settle, parked under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
